This is a personal professional portfolio maintained by Daniel Amlasawole. The underlying APSENS project is developed within VLTHR Lab. This snapshot must not be read as a claim that Daniel personally authored the entire system, model code, validation pipeline or all reported results.

## Dataset and license handling

The v5 report identifies MAFAULDA as a quarantined dataset source and excludes its 1,951 recordings from the CC BY 4.0 commercial training pool in the reported run. This portfolio does not grant any license to third-party datasets. Users must review the original dataset terms, attribution requirements and rights status before reuse.

## Evidence limitations

- Reported validation metrics are lab-level findings and should be interpreted within the methods, splits and constraints described in the validation report.
- A five-of-five QA-gate pass is not proof of live industrial deployment or realized customer ROI.
- Simulated scenarios must not be presented as benchmark-measured results.
- The lab's central repository is the authoritative source for subsequent corrections and new releases.
