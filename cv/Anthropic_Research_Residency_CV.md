## Research Methods & Governance

- **Evidence traceability:** Link public claims to source files, execution run IDs and SHA-256 manifests; classify evidence as benchmark-measured, simulated or requiring live-plant confirmation.
- **Assumption and contradiction registers:** Document assumptions, uncertainties and validator disagreements; exclude unverified agent outputs from permanent project memory.
- **Decision accountability:** Audit Analytical Decision Graphs to distinguish computed outputs, independently verified findings, system recommendations and human decisions.
- **Validation and release governance:** Review dataset provenance, license restrictions, benchmark eligibility and validation evidence; distinguish lab-reported results from personal contributions and from studies that have not yet been run.
