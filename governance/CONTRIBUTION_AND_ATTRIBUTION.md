# Portfolio role and contribution summary

**Role:** Head of Business, Product & Evidence Governance  
**Organization:** VLTHR Lab  
**Effective period:** February 2026 – Present

My role focuses on making research outputs understandable, traceable and appropriately governed for product and commercial decision-making. The contribution includes evidence curation, claims-to-evidence traceability, dataset provenance and licensing oversight, clear separation of measured findings from simulations, and the translation of technical outputs into decision-relevant KPIs.

For APSENS v5, the public release records a validation run containing 2,230 real measured recordings across four source datasets, with 1,951 MAFAULDA recordings quarantined from the CC BY 4.0 commercial pool, zero synthetic rows in the reported run, and five of five hard-stop QA gates passing. These are results reported by VLTHR Lab. My personal contribution relates to the business, product and evidence-governance workstream, not sole technical authorship of those results.
